# Content review

This page lists every place where the site's wording differs from the handout (`reference/QuickStepsWeek1Handouts.pdf`), and every piece of wording that was written fresh. Please read through it and mark anything you want changed. "p." means the handout page.

Where it says "God word", the site swaps in whatever word the person chose (God, Higher Power, or their own). Passages that are direct Big Book quotations are left exactly as printed and never swapped.

## 6. Shared wording for the owner's review

Privacy statement (from the design, near the top of the privacy page):

> Your inventory never leaves your browser. This site has no accounts, no analytics, and no database, and it never receives what you write. Like any website, it's delivered by a hosting provider (Cloudflare), which handles your IP address to deliver the page. We don't collect that information or use it. For the most privacy on a computer, you can download the offline version.

Before launch, please check what your Cloudflare account actually shows you and tell me if this needs adjusting. It deliberately does not claim that IP addresses are not logged.

> This site can't protect you from browser extensions that can read pages, from a device that someone else can open, or from downloaded or printed copies that end up in cloud backups or your Downloads folder.

Not a substitute:

> This site does not replace a sponsor or professional help.

Need support now?

> Need support now?
> If things feel like too much, reach out to your sponsor or someone you trust. In the US, you can call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, at any hour. Elsewhere, please use your local emergency or crisis services.
